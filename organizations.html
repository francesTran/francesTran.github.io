Texas Run Club (TRC)

Member since August 2024 | Website

Texas Run Club is a student organization at the University of Texas at Austin focused on connecting the student running community. The club hosts runs five times a week, and is a great community for runners of all experiences to meet and get faster. Running with TRC since my freshman year played a significant role in the friendships that I've made and my running capabilities.
