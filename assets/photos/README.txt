Put your photos in this folder with these exact names (lowercase).
The ending can be .jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp.

  main.jpg         big photo at the top of the About page
  burrito.jpg      you holding the Cabo Bob's burrito (tall/vertical)
  hardware.jpg     your hardware project (tall/vertical)
  travel.jpg       row of three photos, left
  running.jpg      row of three photos, middle (running / soccer)
  yogurt.jpg       row of three photos, right (Greek yogurt)
  copenhagen.jpg   Just For Fun > Travel > Copenhagen

iPhone photos saved as .HEIC won't show in browsers. Export or convert them to .jpg first.
Then refresh the page (index.html or fun.html) to see them.
